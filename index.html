How it works

  1. 1

    Pick your dates below and see what is open. Reservations are for full days.

  2. 2

    Press a green Reserve button for a van, a MiFi, or both. First come, first served. You get a reservation number right away.

  3. 3

    When you are back, scan the QR code in the van and return the keys to the Admin Suite.

Reserve a van or MiFi

    Modify or Cancel Reservation

    These are your upcoming reservations. A van and MiFi booked together are one reservation, so modifying or cancelling it applies to both.

      Modify lets you pick new dates or a different van or MiFi, and shows what is open. Cancel asks you to confirm first. A trip already under way can't be cancelled here; just return the van and scan the QR code.

      Questions? Email athletics@wlu.edu.
